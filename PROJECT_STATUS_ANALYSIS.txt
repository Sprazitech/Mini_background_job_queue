FUNCTIONAL LIMITATION
---------------------
The current send_email worker handler is simulated. It waits and returns success;
it does not send a real email through SMTP, Resend, SendGrid, Mailgun, Amazon SES,
or another provider. A successful send_email job currently confirms only that the
simulated handler completed.
